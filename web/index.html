The job, in four moves

01 · Seal

Turn the dial to your max. Your browser encrypts a yes/no for every price level. The number never leaves this page.

02 · Bond

A small shielded bond, with a memo that ties it to your sealed ballot and a one-time refund address.

03 · Crack

At close, a 2-of-3 committee opens only the sums a binary search needs. Never a single bid.

04 · Settle

Winners swap ZEC for the item in one atomic Ironwood transaction. Bonds come back.

No trackers, no third-party scripts. Whoever serves these files (a Tally bulletin, or GitHub Pages for the static demo) sees your IP like any web host; the Tally bulletin keeps no request logs. Use Tor if that matters to you.
