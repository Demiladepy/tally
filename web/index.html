How a sealed block auction works

Same steps as any primary allocation. None of the leakage.
01

Submit a sealed order

Set your limit price on the order ticket. Your browser encrypts it before it leaves the page; the desk receives ciphertext and a signed receipt.

02

Fund the deposit

A small refundable deposit in shielded ZEC commits the order. Only depositors count at clearing.

03

Single clearing price

At close, a 2-of-3 committee opens only the aggregate totals a binary search needs. Everyone who clears pays the same price.

04

Atomic settlement

Each allocation settles delivery-versus-payment in one Ironwood transaction. Deposits are returned.

For sellers

Run your own sealed sale from the browser

Set the terms, have a three-member committee co-sign them, publish, and close the book when the window ends. Your seller key is created in your tab and never sent anywhere.

Open the seller console

Built for desks that can't afford to leak

No order is ever revealed

Not to the seller, the operator or the committee. Only a handful of aggregate totals are opened, never an individual bid.

Verifiable, not trusted

Every clearing ships a report anyone can re-run in the browser: signatures, proofs, decryptions, price and allocations.

Shielded end to end

Deposits, payment and delivery move only in Zcash's Ironwood pool. No transparent addresses, no published counterparties.
