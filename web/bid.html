One price for everyone. Every order that clears pays the same clearing price: the highest price level at which demand still covers the units offered. Your limit is a ceiling, not what you pay.

Your limit stays sealed. The ticket turns your limit into an encrypted yes/no at each price level, with zero-knowledge proofs that it is well formed. No one, including the desk, can read it.

Only aggregates are opened. At close the committee opens only the few aggregate totals a binary search needs to find the clearing level. If there are more winners than units at that level, ties are broken by a public random seed.

Deposits. Each order needs a small refundable deposit in shielded ZEC. Allocated orders that don't settle forfeit it to a forfeit address, never to the seller. Everyone else is refunded.

every level stays sealed unless the search needs its total

This offering was co-signed by every member of the clearing committee. Your browser checks each signature before it will seal an order.
