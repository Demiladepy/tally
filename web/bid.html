Your max —

Turn the dial to the most you'd pay.

If the auction clears at or below your max, you win and pay the clearing price, the same as every other winner. Above it, you don't win and your bond comes back.

Your number never leaves this tab. The browser encrypts a yes/no for each of the price levels; only that ciphertext and its proofs go to the bulletin.

Post the bond.

A small shielded payment to the escrow. It keeps bids honest: winners who don't pay lose it, to a forfeit address, never to the seller. Everyone else gets it back.
